Welcome to ViewFinder!

About ViewFinder

We are working on a computational media monitoring framework for India, and the current version of this tool demonstrates this framework in action. By scraping articles and applying language processing techniques, we arrive at the political tone of an article.

We first identify entities belonging to establishment (EST) or opposition (OPP) alliances. EST (OPP) entities include people, parties, and organizations allied or affiliated with the ruling (opposition) coalition.

The political tone of a statement describes the sentiment (positve/negative/neutral) towards EST (OPP) entities in that sentence. We aggragate this across sentences in an article to arrive at the article's political tone. We analyse the political tone of past articles for an outlet to determine its reporting preference.

Getting Started

Navigate to your extensions menu (the puzzle piece icon) on the toolbar and click ViewFinder. On most pages, this will display a popup menu. Here, you'll be able to choose whether to fetch political tone for EST entities or OPP entities. The green "Fetch" button activates on Google News Full Coverage pages. To access one of these pages, navigate to the India topic in Google News. Choose any political event from this list, and click See more headlines and perspectives. This should take you to a cluster of articles about the event. This is the Full Coverage page.

Clicking ViewFinder again from the extension menu should show a popup with the "Fetch" button active. You can click it to begin extracting political tone.

Usage

Once the page loads, you'll see a Circle and a Square next to each article's outlet.

The colour of the Circle represents the political tone of the current article (green: positive, yellow: neutral, red: negative). On clicking the Circle, you will be able to view the sentence level political tone annotations for this article.

The colour of the Square represents the overall reporting preference of the outlet (green: positive, yellow: neutral, red: negative). On clicking the Square, you will be able to visualise and interact with the political tone of past articles, and observe the evolution in reporting preference over time.

If either of these icons are gray, it means we were unable to collect data for that article/outlet.
