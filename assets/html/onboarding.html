Welcome to ViewFinder!

About ViewFinder

We are working on a computational media monitoring framework for India, and the current version of this tool demonstrates this framework in action. By scraping articles and applying language processing techniques, we arrive at the political tone of an article.

We first identify entities belonging to establishment (EST) or opposition (OPP) alliances. EST (OPP) entities include people, parties, and organizations allied or affiliated with the ruling (opposition) coalition.

The political tone of a sentence describes the sentiment of a sentence towards EST (OPP) entities in that sentence. We aggragate this across sentences in an article to arrive at the article's political tone. We count the political tone of past articles over time for certain chosen outlets to arrive at an outlet level tone.

Getting Started

Navigate to your extensions menu (the puzzle piece icon) on the toolbar and click ViewFinder. On most pages, this will display a popup menu. Here, you'll be able to choose whether to fetch political tone for EST entities or OPP entities. This option can be changed.

After this, navigate to the India topic in Google News. Choose any political event from this list, and click See more headlines and perspectives. This should take you to a cluster of articles about the event.

Now, from the extensions menu, click ViewFinder again. This time, instead of the popup menu, you'll see a loading screen.

Usage

Once the page loads, you'll see a Circle and a Square next to each article's outlet.

The colour of the Circle represents the political tone of the current article (green: positive, yellow: neutral, red: negative). On clicking the Circle, you will be able to view the sentence level political tone annotations for this article.

The colour of the Square represents the overall political tone of the outlet (green: positive, yellow: neutral, red: negative). On clicking the Square, you will be able to interact with the counts of articles and their political tone for some time range, and how this evolves over time.

If either of these icons are gray, it means we were unable to collect data for that article/outlet.
